## Interpretation

The repository can reproduce the reported item counts and the archived visual
files, but it cannot recreate every network layout exactly from the available
materials. The manuscript therefore avoids claims that depend on exact node
positions or cross-period comparison of absolute total link strength. The two
temporal networks are interpreted within their own subcorpora because corpus size
and network density differ.
